## Introduction

- A mobile app to explore and compare GDP in Malaysia, by state and by economic sector
- The user picks an analysis mode, picks the states and sector, and the app shows one chart with one simple sentence to explain it
- We focus on the industrial and manufacturing side of state GDP, which links to SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure)
- All data comes from data.gov.my, the official government open data

## Problem and Solution

| Problem | Our Solution |
|---------|--------------|
| Official GDP data is made for desktop websites and long PDF reports | We rebuild the same official data as a mobile-first app |
| Official views are fixed - users cannot mix and match states, sectors and years | No typing - just tap filter chips to pick states, sector and year, every choice gives a valid result |
| Reports use many technical terms, hard for a normal person to read | Every answer is one chart plus one plain sentence, e.g. "Penang's manufacturing output is 2.1 times Kedah's" |
| No mobile app lets a normal person compare the data by themselves | 4 self-service analysis modes: compare states, sector breakdown, trend over years, sector dependence check |

- Extra: the data is cached in the app, so it still works without internet

- Our app turns official but hard-to-read GDP data into simple, mobile-friendly answers
